# Privacy Policy — NFL Scores

**Effective date:** [set when you publish]

NFL Scores ("the app") is a Reddit Developer Platform (Devvit) app that posts live National Football League game-thread scoreboards in the subreddits that install it. This policy explains what data the app uses and, just as importantly, what it does not.

## What the app does not collect

The app does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information about Reddit users. It does not track individuals, build user profiles, run analytics, or use advertising trackers.

## Data the app uses to function

- **Public NFL data.** The app fetches publicly available game data — scores, box scores, play-by-play, drive summaries, team and player statistics, injury reports, win probability, and publicly posted betting lines — from ESPN's public sports API (site.api.espn.com). No user information is sent to retrieve this data.
- **Images.** Team logos are bundled with the app. Player headshots and highlight thumbnails are retrieved from ESPN's public image servers (a.espncdn.com, espnmedia-cdn.akamaized.net, media.video-cdn.espn.com) through the app's own server, which requests only the image and passes along no information about the person viewing it.
- **Fonts.** The app loads typography from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) to render its interface.
- **Reddit platform data.** To post and update game threads, the app uses the subreddit and post identifiers provided by Reddit's Developer Platform, acting within the community that installed it.
- **Subreddit settings.** Configuration a moderator chooses — such as which team the community follows and whether postgame threads post automatically — is stored with the installation. These are community settings, not personal data.
- **Internal mappings.** The app stores small technical records in Reddit-provided storage: which NFL game each post corresponds to, and short-lived keys that prevent duplicate posts. These records contain game identifiers and Reddit post/subreddit identifiers only — no personal user information — and expire automatically (duplicate-prevention keys within a few days; post-to-game mappings within about 180 days).
- **Local display preferences.** Your chosen scoreboard theme (light or dark) is saved in your own browser. It stays on your device and is not transmitted to or collected by the app.

## Third-party services

The app retrieves data from ESPN's public API and image servers and from Google Fonts. Data those services provide is subject to their own policies. The app does not share any data with them beyond the standard request needed to load public content. Highlight clips, where available, open on ESPN's own platform, which is governed by ESPN's policies rather than this one.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
