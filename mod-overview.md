# MLB Scoreboards — for Moderators

**Turn your game threads into a live, interactive scoreboard your community watches the game around.**

It's free, it's running now, and it takes about two minutes to set up.

👉 **[Install it](https://developers.reddit.com/apps/mlb-scores)** · 👀 **[See it live on r/MLBScoreboards](https://www.reddit.com/r/MLBScoreboards/)** (best during a game)

---

## What your community gets

A game thread that's actually *live*. During the game, the thread shows a real scoreboard that updates pitch by pitch:

- **Score, count, and base/outs** — the whole situation at a glance
- **Full box score** — both lineups, batting and pitching
- **Every play** — with exit velocity, launch angle, and distance
- **Win probability** — a live chart your fans can tap to see the play behind each swing

Your community watches it update in real time and talks through the game around it — all in one thread. It's the game thread they already love, just live.

---

## You're in control — pick how it fits your sub

**Option 1 — Make it your game threads.** It posts and runs your game threads for you. When a game ends, the postgame thread posts automatically.

**Option 2 — Run it as a companion** *(Broadcast mode)*. Keep your current game threads exactly as they are, and add this alongside as a live second screen for the fans who want it. In this mode, **it never posts anything on its own** — only the threads you choose to post. Nothing changes about your sub unless you want it to.

Either way, it's your call — and you can switch anytime with one setting.

---

## Setup is genuinely simple

1. **[Install it](https://developers.reddit.com/apps/mlb-scores)** on your sub.
2. In settings, **pick your team** (or "All Teams" for a league-wide sub).
3. Hit **"Post today's game threads"** from the mod menu.

That's it. From there it runs itself — you post the day's threads with one click, and each one goes live on its own.

---

## No babysitting

Set your team once and it handles the season — every game type, doubleheaders, postponements, the postseason, all automatically. No copy-pasting scores, no manual updates. Just run the menu on game days.
